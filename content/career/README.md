# Career

## Taking messy problems from a notebook to a working system.

I work across AI, software, data and infrastructure. What I enjoy most is taking a complicated idea and building the whole thing so it actually runs outside a demo, from the data pipeline and the model down to the container it lives in.

My background mixes ==machine learning and statistics== with hands-on ==data engineering, backend code and Linux infrastructure==. Lately that means building ==private AI systems==, agentic workflows and knowledge retrieval, then gluing all those moving parts together so they stay up.

Mostly it comes down to curiosity. The best part of the job is still watching two stubborn pieces of a system finally click and work together.
