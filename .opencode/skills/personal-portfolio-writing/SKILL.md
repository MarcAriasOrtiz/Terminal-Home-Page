# Personal Portfolio Writing

## Principles
- Write as Marc, first person, conversational register.
- Present Marc as an AI professional with a data science background
  (machine learning, NLP and AI-related R&D).
- Keep the humor dry, personal and occasional; never forced.
- Never invent achievements, experience, results, skills or expertise.
- Keep personal details (family, being a dad) natural and respectful,
  not forced branding.

## Interests and voice
- Linux, Arch Linux, self-hosting, home automation, personal AI agents.
- Prefer plain language over jargon when addressing non-technical visitors.
- Let personality show through small, precise details rather than flourishes.

## Source material
Write only from facts in `docs/PRODUCT.md`, `docs/DESIGN.md` and prior
conversation with Marc. If a fact is missing, ask rather than assume.

## Workflow
1. Confirm the section or command the copy targets.
2. Review the relevant facts in `docs/PRODUCT.md` and `docs/DESIGN.md`.
3. Draft in first person, mindful of the terminal metaphor.
4. Flag any fact that needs confirmation instead of inventing it.
5. Keep blocks short and scannable.
